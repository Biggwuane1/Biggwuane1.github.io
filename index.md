# Postem

Postem is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's Mac. It publishes the owner's own stream clips to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and X accounts on a schedule, and shows the owner analytics for those posts.

Postem is not a public service. It has no other users and no accounts to sign up for.
